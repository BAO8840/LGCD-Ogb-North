    About Ogbomoso North

    Created
    27 September 1991, from the former Ogbomoso Local Government
    State
    Oyo State, Federal Republic of Nigeria
    Languages
    Yoruba and English
    Major markets
    Wazo, Ayanyan and Jagun
    Institutions
    Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), LAUTECH Teaching Hospital, Bowen University Teaching Hospital, Nigerian Baptist Medical Centre
    Traditional seat
    The Soun’s Palace, Ogbomoso
