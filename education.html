    Education

    Information on schools, enrolment and learning support in Ogbomoso North.

    What the council does

    Primary education

    The Local Government takes part in providing and maintaining primary education, together with Oyo State.

    Adult and vocational learning

    Adult literacy and vocational training help residents gain skills for work.

    School facilities

    Classrooms, water and toilets in public schools need regular care. Tell us about any fault.

    Enrol a child

    1. Contact the councilCall, send a WhatsApp message or visit the office.
    2. Prepare documentsBirth certificate or other proof of age, and the parent or guardian’s phone number.
    3. Choose a schoolPick a school near your home.
    4. Confirm admissionConfirm with the head teacher of the school.

    Schools in Ogbomoso North

    Higher education

    Ogbomoso North is home to the main campus of Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH). Students, NYSC members and researchers who wish to serve or study with the Local Government can write to the council.

    Common questions

    Where do I report a school problem?

    Call or message the council and give the name of the school and the ward.

    What if my child has no birth certificate?

    The Local Government registers births. Contact the council to ask how to register or obtain a certificate.
