    Health centre standards

    A functional Level 2 primary health centre should provide:

    • 24-hour service
    • A skilled birth attendant
    • Reliable electricity with solar backup
    • Clean water and sanitation
    • Consulting and delivery rooms
    • A pharmacy and laboratory
    • Secure premises

    How we maintain public assets

    1. InspectWard teams check the condition of each asset.
    2. PlanRepairs are ranked by safety and use.
    3. FundUpkeep has its own line in the budget.
    4. FixA contractor or council team does the work.
    5. VerifyThe repair is checked and recorded.
