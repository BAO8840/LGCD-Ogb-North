    How council projects are planned, approved and delivered, and how you can take part.

    How a project moves

    1. ProposedA need is identified by residents, ward leaders or departments.
    2. ApprovedThe council reviews and approves the project and its budget.
    3. In progressA contractor or council team carries out the work.
    4. CompletedThe work is inspected and the record is published.
